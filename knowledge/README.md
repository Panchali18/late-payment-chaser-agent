# Knowledge

[`payment-terms.md`](payment-terms.md) is the one-page standard payment terms for Kestrel Lane Print & Signage. It is uploaded to the agent as a knowledge source and is the **only** place late-fee wording may come from.

It is written to agree with the chasing rules: the 2% late fee applies from 14 days overdue (the Formal stage), only for clients whose agreement doesn't exclude it, and queried or promised invoices are not chased.
