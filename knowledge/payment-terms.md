# Kestrel Lane Print & Signage Pty Ltd — Standard Payment Terms

*Fictional business. For demonstration only.*

These terms apply to all business customers unless a client's written agreement says otherwise.

## 1. Payment period
1.1 Invoices are payable within **30 days** of the invoice date, unless a different period is agreed in writing for a client.
1.2 The due date shown on each invoice is the date payment must be received.

## 2. How to pay
2.1 Payment is accepted by bank transfer using the invoice number as the payment reference. Bank details are shown on each invoice.
2.2 Invoices are issued in the client's agreed currency. Payment must be made in the same currency.

## 3. Reminders
3.1 We send a courtesy reminder before an invoice falls due, and further reminders if it becomes overdue.
3.2 If you believe an invoice is incorrect, or you have a question about it, please contact our accounts team. We will pause reminders on that invoice while we look into it.
3.3 If you have agreed a payment date with us, we will not send reminders before that date.

## 4. Late payment fee
4.1 Where an invoice remains unpaid **14 days or more** after its due date, we may charge a late payment fee of **2% of the overdue amount**.
4.2 The late payment fee does not apply to clients whose written agreement excludes it.
4.3 We will always tell you before a late payment fee is added to your account.

## 5. Part payments
5.1 Where part of an invoice has been paid, reminders state the remaining balance only.
